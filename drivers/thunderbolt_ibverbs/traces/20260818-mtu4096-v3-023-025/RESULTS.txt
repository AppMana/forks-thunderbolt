   kernel.hung_task_panic=1 (X670 watchdog rig) turned the deliberate wait into a panic.
   tbrxe has no disassociate_ucontext (untracked remap_vmalloc_range mmaps, same as

   teardown on these nodes; every bench now runs under timeout(1) and is pkilled.
