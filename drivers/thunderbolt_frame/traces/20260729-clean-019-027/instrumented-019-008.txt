The -ENOMEM is tbv_path_reserve_data() refusing the reservation: a 1 MiB WR is
260 frames, the per-path TX queue holds ~1024, and the QP advertises a send
queue of 128 WRs. The SQ was nowhere near full (4 outstanding on that QP) --
the driver is surfacing an internal per-path frame budget as a synchronous
post_send failure, which RC verbs consumers treat as fatal.
