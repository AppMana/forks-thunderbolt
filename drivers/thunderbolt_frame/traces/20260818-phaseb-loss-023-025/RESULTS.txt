Per-stage budget (tbrxe-lat-timeline --events + analyzer, lat-events-*.txt):
  all software stages single-digit us (post->sender ~3-4, sender->xmit ~2,
  xmit->enq ~0.3, rxirq->work ~3, work->clirx ~0.7, clirx->receiver ~2.4-2.9,
  recv->ackxmit ~1.6-1.9, cq->post ~1-2)
  enq_to_txirq med 20-74 us p99 132 us  <- MSI-X moderation, direct measurement
  recv_to_cq p99 163 us                 <- same moderation on the completion side
NHI throttle regs: 128 us on ALL 16 vectors both nodes (nhi_enable_int_throttling).
Legacy driver wrote 0 (nhi_interrupt_throttle_ns default 0 via tb_ring_throttling).
Legacy committed record: bench/results/strix-2p-noiommu-2x40g (2026-05-27):
  ib_write_lat 7.41 us typ, ib_send_lat 8.34 us typ, ib_read_lat 15.37 us (64B).
Attribution: 2-3 interrupt-moderated crossings/RTT at up to 128 us each explain
essentially the whole legacy-vs-now delta; software budget sums ~25-35 us/RTT.

== Bandwidth matrix (ib_write_bw 1MiB, BW average Gb/s, unprobed)
  q1  uni    8.13      q32 uni    8.60
  q1  bidir 11.56      q8  bidir 15.53   q16 bidir 12.98   q32 bidir 12.21
Owner reference: single-QP 1MiB write 973 MB/s = 8.14 Gb/s (matches q1-uni).
CPU: no core saturated (max per-cpu 44% busy); rxe_wq spread over many workers.
Frame-rate ceiling: 8.6 Gb/s / 2048 B = ~525k frames/s unidirectional regardless
of QP count; legacy 16 Gb/s uni at 4 KiB frames = ~500k frames/s. The NHI/fabric
moves ~0.5M frames/s per direction independent of frame size; bytes/frame is the
lever, not QPs and not CPU.

== Ranked levers (bandwidth/latency)
1. data_ring_throttle_ns=0 (new tbframe knob; legacy parity). Predicted: RTT
   typ from ~65-142 us to ~15-30 us; possible q1 bw gain via ACK RTT; expected
   ~no change to q32 uni (frame-rate ceiling) -- the A/B discriminates.
2. IB_MTU_4096 deviation (spec s5 documented lever): 4012 B payload/frame at
   ~500k frames/s -> ~16-17 Gb/s uni predicted. Code change in tbrxe (report
   MTU 4096, fragment at 4012); both ends fork-consistent.
3. RC retransmit-timeout cap (legacy 0.2.17 analog) for the CRC-loss tail
   (100x iterations); does not raise average bw.
4. rxe_wq wakeup chain (~10-15 us/RTT across 3 hops) - pinning/inline; only
   after 1-2 land.
